How I design

  • Problem before pixelsI don’t open Figma until we agree on the problem. On Beacon, that meant narrowing “the whole supply chain” to the purchase order.
  • Make the thinking visibleJourney maps, story maps and system models give a room something to point at. A configuration map unblocked NGPOS when meetings couldn’t.
  • Design is a team sportGood design needs buy-in from stakeholders, product, engineering and leadership. I build those relationships early, so decisions stick.
  • Stay curious, keep shippingI learn new tools by using them on real work. Right now that’s AI: Figma Make prototypes, research synthesis and front ends built with Claude Code.

About me

I’m a product designer in Cincinnati with 15+ years in banking, B2B e-commerce and retail supply chain. Leading a team taught me to judge design work by how a designer frames the problem, not just by the screens at the end.

My favorite part of the job is the messy middle: the whiteboards, the workshops and the moment a room finally agrees on how a process should work. Then I make it real, in Figma and more and more in code.

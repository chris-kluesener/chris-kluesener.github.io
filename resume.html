About me

Design leader and hands-on product designer with 15+ years building digital products, from the first native mobile banking apps at a top-20 US bank to establishing product design from scratch in Kroger’s supply chain organization. Built and ran a team of about 7 designers, directed the design of greenfield MVPs toward an end-to-end supply chain control tower (Beacon), and most recently led design of a greenfield rollout platform for NextGen Point-of-Sale, using AI across the workflow, from research synthesis to Figma Make prototypes to front-end code built with Claude Code. Strongest where the problem is ambiguous and the answer isn’t a screen yet: discovery, systems thinking, and aligning product and engineering on what to build.
